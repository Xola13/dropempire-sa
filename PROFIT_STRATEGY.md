# DropEmpire SA — Profit Strategy Guide

## Business Model Overview

DropEmpire SA is a **dropshipping** business. You never hold inventory.
When a customer orders, you forward the order to the supplier who ships directly to the customer.

**Your profit = Customer price − Supplier cost − Shipping cost**

---

## Supplier Strategy

### 1. AliExpress (High Margin, Long Delivery)
- **Use for:** Unique products, trending gadgets, accessories
- **Typical cost:** R50–R300
- **Target selling price:** 2.5x–4x cost
- **Margin:** 60–75%
- **Delivery:** 15–25 days
- **Risk:** Customer patience required; returns can be difficult

### 2. CJ Dropshipping (Balanced)
- **Use for:** Electronics, fashion, home goods
- **Typical cost:** R80–R500
- **Target selling price:** 2x–3x cost
- **Margin:** 40–60%
- **Delivery:** 7–15 days
- **Risk:** MOQ on some products; needs API setup

### 3. Makro SA (Speed Play)
- **Use for:** Bulky/local items, trust signal
- **Typical cost:** R100–R1,000
- **Target selling price:** 1.3x–1.8x cost (lower margins)
- **Margin:** 20–35%
- **Delivery:** 2–4 days
- **Risk:** Lower margins; Makro can undercut you directly

---

## Pricing Rules

### Minimum Margin: 20%
The system enforces this. No product is listed or fulfilled with < 20% margin.

### Target Margin by Supplier
| Supplier | Minimum | Target | Stretch |
|----------|---------|--------|---------|
| AliExpress | 20% | 60% | 75% |
| CJ | 20% | 45% | 60% |
| Makro | 20% | 28% | 35% |

### Pricing Formula
```
Selling Price = Cost Price / (1 - Target Margin %)

Example: Cost R100, target 40% margin:
Price = 100 / (1 - 0.40) = R166.67 → round to R169.99
```

## Product Selection Strategy

### High-Demand Categories (SA Market)
1. **Gadgets & Electronics** — Always trending, high AOV
2. **Home & Kitchen** — High repeat purchase rate
3. **Pet Supplies** — Emotional buyers, repeat customers
4. **Fitness & Sports** — Seasonal spikes, January & June
5. **Baby Products** — Loyal customers, word-of-mouth

### Product Scoring Criteria
- Demand score > 70/100 (Claude AI rates this)
- Margin > 35%
- Shipping days < 20 (prefer CJ or Makro)
- No branded/counterfeit items
- Weight < 2kg (avoid heavy shipping costs)

### Red Flags (Avoid These)
- Electronics with brand logos (legal risk)
- Food, supplements, cosmetics (SAHPRA regulations)
- Clothing sized S/M/L (sizing complaints)
- Fragile items (breakage during SA delivery)

## Cash Flow Management

Since you pay suppliers AFTER customers pay you:
- **PayFast settles:** Next business day (instant EFT)
- **Yoco settles:** 1-2 business days
- **You pay supplier:** Within 24h of payment confirmation

This means **zero working capital needed** for inventory.

---

## Risk Management

### Price Creep (Supplier Cost Increases)
- Daily price sync monitors for cost changes
- If margin drops below 20%, product is flagged for review
- System never fulfills if current margin is negative

### Delivery Delays
- Always show honest estimates (15-25d AliExpress, 7-15d CJ)
- Proactive email update at 15 days if not shipped
- Offer partial refund after 30 days if not delivered

### Returns & Refunds
- Full refund if item doesn't arrive within stated window
- Quality photos required for "not as described" disputes
- Most AliExpress suppliers offer refunds on documented disputes
